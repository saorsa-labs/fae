## CRITICAL FINDINGS (5+ votes required to block)

### [CRITICAL — 5 votes] Pre-state capture happens AFTER tool execution
**Reviewers**: quality-patterns, codex, kimi, glm, minimax (5/15)
**Issue**: `createReceipt()` is called at step 16 in `ToolExecutor.executeInner()`, AFTER `tool.execute()` at step 14. `captureFilePreState(atPath: path)` therefore reads the file AFTER it has been overwritten by write/edit tools. The stored blob is the POST-mutation state, not pre-mutation state. `undo()` would "restore" the already-mutated content — non-functional for file tools.
**Fix required**: Move pre-state capture to BEFORE step 14. Simplest fix: split into pre-capture before step 14 and receipt commit after (only on success), or simply call `createReceipt()` before `tool.execute()` (receipts for failed calls are harmless waste).
**BLOCKING: YES — undo() is the core value proposition of this phase.**
