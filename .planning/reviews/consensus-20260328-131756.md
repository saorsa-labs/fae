### MUST FIX (1 finding, 1 vote — below threshold but high UX impact)

| Countdown "Cancelled." speech races with barge-in audio processing | 4 | Error-Handling, Codex, Kimi, GLM, MiniMax, Quality-Patterns |

> **Verdict: SHOULD FIX** (4+ votes — threshold met). The `speakDirect("Cancelled.")` call in `toolExecutorCountdownBeforeIrreversible` fires when `bargeInState.pendingBargeIn != nil`, but the barge-in pipeline is simultaneously processing the user's audio. This can produce double-speech or speech after a new turn starts. **Fix: remove the `speakDirect("Cancelled.")` call and return `false` immediately.** The barge-in audio will be processed naturally as the user's cancellation.
